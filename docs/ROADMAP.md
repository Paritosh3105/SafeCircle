# Step-by-step v1 implementation

## Step 1 — complete in this package

Monorepo, Next.js web, Fastify versioned API, shared schemas, PostgreSQL migration,
private access/recovery, encrypted synthetic requests and pending applications.

## Step 2 — verification and staff identity

Step 2A implements separate operator-only staff provisioning, password login,
expiring staff sessions and role-gated status workspaces. See STAFF_AUTH.md for
setup, verification and authentication work still required before live use.

Next: private credential review,
identity/register/institution cross-checks, role-specific scopes, reviewed decisions,
appeals, periodic checks, expiry/suspension and active-case reassignment.
Do not rely on uploaded certificates or self-selected roles.

## Step 3 — real private support

Actual programme hours and capacity, free-session limits before queues, human
triage, eligible assignment, scope/consent checks, private text conversation,
reassignment and status transitions. Implement safeguarding policy and trained
review before accepting live disclosures. Record notice versions and private reads.

## Step 4 — specialist appointments and referrals

Legal conflict checks, clinical service requirements, specific disclosure preview
and consent, professional booking confirmation and real external adapters.
Keep receipt confirmation separate from successful delivery of assistance.

## Step 5 — private tools

Text incident timeline, optional private journal, survivor-led safety plan, complaint
drafts, deliberate export, retention/deletion and backup policy. Private notes require
separate sharing permission. No automatic public posts or police complaints.

## Step 6 — community and mobile

Moderated age-eligible text spaces with no member messaging. English/Hindi content
and accessibility testing. React Native/Expo native screens, secure storage and
reviewed native authentication. Push starts disabled with neutral opt-in content.

UI discussions can change the visual design without moving permissions or consent
rules into the browser. Foundations and operational staffing come before live launch.
